# Domain-child convergence arc — plan

## Context

PR #38 (RS-1 … RS-6, CI green) landed the durable machinery for domain specialisation: a judged coverage verdict beside fitness, domain children minted under matched shape classes with parent+label identity, the waterfall render, retrieval/harvest reach, a spec covering all twenty obligations. Exact repeats converge (identity stable 18/21). The convergence sweep of 2026-09-16 (`development/bench/ood-stress-results/2026-09-16_093519-rs6-convergence-sweep/FINDINGS.md`) then showed the realistic case does NOT: 24 paraphrases in 8 groups → 0 landings; every off-domain group minted 2–3 children; one in-domain legal paraphrase minted a mis-parented child; behaviors converged 56/56. Mechanisms: the parent shape moves between paraphrases while identity is parent-scoped; labels are coined at instance granularity even with the sibling shown. Also observed: a newborn child was reached by the next task and a grandchild minted under it; a child in the 1–2 occurrence band gets no child line; the child's accrued strengths are invisible before consolidation; an unquoted `:code` closure burned 5 iterations.

User direction: merge #38, then immediately grill (`/grill-with-docs`, grounded in every prior finding) and slice the follow-up so the feature is faithful and useful before anything else, and PROVE convergence on a large realistic-traffic sample rather than claim it. Decisions taken: LLM-generated corpus from reviewed briefs (ground truth from the brief, never the model's label); ~400 classify-only tasks × 2 passes + ~30 end-to-end campaigns; D3 (parent-scoped identity) and D7 (label among siblings) are open to revision.

## Part A — realistic-traffic experiment (RS-7)

**Corpus** `development/bench/ood-corpus-traffic/`: `briefs.edn` (40 hand-written briefs: 4 in-domain — document-analysis, risk-analysis, contract-comparison, legal-issue-detection with their seed ids — 2 near-domain confounders marked `:confounder-of`, 34 off-domain incl. the 6 existing groups; fields `:brief-id :group :in-domain? :expected-seed-id :subject-matter :material :output-kind :instance-space :must-not-be`), `tasks/NNN-<group>-vVV.txt` (3-digit prefix, seeded round-robin interleave, header for humans only), `manifest.edn` (slug → ground truth, generator provenance with prompt sha and usage, review status, flags; `corpus-sha256` over accepted entries, asserted by every run), `REVIEW.md`.

**Generator** `development/src/traffic_corpus_gen.clj`: `runner/register-models!` then `ai.obney.orc.llm.interface/predict :openrouter` with `:with-metadata? true` (module shape as `gepa/core/todo_processors.clj` `make-llm-fn`); two calls per brief × 5 variants, a fixed style card per variant (ticket / long email / bullet spec with explicit schema / conversational / numbered / inline data excerpt) varying length 60–600 words and output contract; prior instances passed as "do not reuse"; the prompt never contains the group, brief id or any label. Review flags (structured, never a metric): within-group token-set Jaccard > 0.6 (a repeat, not a paraphrase), cross-group > 0.35 (ambiguous truth), ColBERT nearest neighbour from another group (`colbert/rerank`, no index), in-domain variant missing the brief's output kind. Human spot-check per `REVIEW.md`; rejected slugs excluded. ~0.3M tokens.

**Harness** `development/src/rs7_traffic_sweep.clj` (rs6 stays untouched): reuses `rs6-specialisation-sweep/classify-one!` (add an optional ctx-extra arg carrying `::task-slug`), `ood/load-corpus`, `runner/start!`. Per-record atomic persistence (`.tmp` + ATOMIC_MOVE) with resume (skip `:ok`, `:retry-errors?`), per-task 180 s future timeout, events snapshot every 25 tasks + `restore!` (`runner/start!` → append snapshot → `drive-projectors!` made public → `force-rebuild!`); `install-tee!` via alter-var-root on the orc execute var and `reranker/rerank!` to capture `:usage`, `:rerank-calls`, timeouts per slug; `:index-id-at-classify`, `:newborn-in-index?`, `:reached-via` ∈ graph-land / walk-down / index-match / seed-match / mint / bundle / deferred; `run.edn` manifest (commit, dirty flag, corpus sha, task order, reindex policy, parallelism, models, thresholds, jvm args) + `tree-before.edn`/`tree-after.edn` (every tree-class concept with label and broader). Reindex policy option `:none | {:every-k K} | :processor` (`runner/start-reindex-processor!` for the faithful path); **use `{:every-k 25}` for both arms** (deterministic, exercises index-match; ~16 rebuilds ≈ 50 min/pass) and `:processor` once on the smoke subset. **Sequential** (the read-then-mint decision has no lock; concurrent same-group tasks would scatter by construction); optional `:parallelism 2` only with a group-aware scheduler, never for the comparison.

**Metrics** (`analyse`, pure over records + manifest; ground truth = manifest group): per group child-count (target 1 off-domain, 0 in-domain), landing-rate on 2nd+ decided variants, wrong-parent rate and in-domain mint count (target 0), confounder mints under a seed, cross-parent scatter (distinct parents; distinct top-1 targets), label-variant count, converged? strict and relaxed ≥0.8; corpus-wide deferrals per axis and reason, behavioral fresh-mint rate, reached-via frequencies, newborn-in-index rate at landing, tokens and latency (mean/median/p95/total), pass-1 vs pass-2 identity stability and majority-child landing. `compare-runs [a b]` asserts equal corpus sha and order → `COMPARISON.md`.

**E2E subset** (`run-e2e!`): 15 groups (8 original + 2 new off-domain + 4 in-domain + 1 confounder) × v01 and last accepted variant, fresh store, v01s first; reuses `run-full-bench-observation!` mechanics + `rs6-usefulness-bench/child-state`; records status, usage, classified/minted/occurrence/claim events (are verdict and claims on the CHILD id?), render candidates and prepend (as data), child-after-run, tree node count. ~1 h, ~3M tokens per arm.

**Cost**: per arm ≈ 2 × 2.8 h classify + 1 h e2e ≈ 7 h, ~23M tokens; both arms ≈ 14 h, ~46M tokens.

## Part B — the grill agenda (ordered; recommendation in brackets)

Q0 What "converged" means → one **domain family** per domain per tenant, under whichever shape first minted it. Glossary: Domain family.
Q1 Identity (D3) → keep parent+label DERIVATION for the first mint (existing identities valid), LAND by canonical label tenant-wide; "one domain under two shapes" moves to the judge (Q3). ADR 0007 supersedes ADR 0006's lookup clause, not its derivation.
Q2 Label granularity (D7) → task-family instruction text plus a bounded tenant-wide label list shown to the reranker (new blackboard slot); cheap, but the proof must not rest on prose calibration.
Q3 Judged merge on a would-be mint → nearest existing families by rank (label + birth signature neighbourhood), one discrete "same family as X / new / unknown" question, reason before verdict; same → land, new → mint, unknown → defer; gated by prototype RS-P3; zero calls on landings and covered matches.
Q4 Covered-seed protection → a `covered` CHILDLESS tree-class candidate at/above the existing match threshold wins over a partial top-1 that would mint; `:domain-selection` recorded; runs BEFORE family landing (a mis-parented family must not capture later in-domain tasks). No new threshold.
Q5 A domain child is a leaf on the domain axis → a match or walk-down resolving to a domain child is a LANDING (parent = its skos:broader), verdict not consulted; no grandchildren; the child line renders because the outcome is a domain outcome.
Q6 Zero band → at total 0 a newborn (agent-authored provenance) is matchable but NOT surfaced until the gate; curated seeds still pass; matching stays ungated (CV-1 deadlock).
Q7 Render → child strengths whenever present; closure repair hint keyed on the ex-data keyword / shared constant (reactive `diagnose-parse-error` arm + preventive pitfalls bullet).
Q8 Proof shape (D6) → deterministic tests on structured data are the gate; the RS-7 sweeps report metrics, never thresholds-as-gates.
