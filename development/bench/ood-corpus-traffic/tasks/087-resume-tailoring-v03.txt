Please generate a tailored application package for a High School English Teacher position at a Tokyo-based international school, transitioning from a US public school background. Use the provided CV and job description to produce an output strictly adhering to the following schema:

• REVISED CV: A restructured version of my resume that highlights IB curriculum alignment and international pedagogy. Formatting must be professional and localized for the Japanese private school market.
• CHANGE LOG: A detailed table documenting all significant edits. Columns should include: Original Phrasing, Revised Phrasing, and Strategic Rationale.
• COVER LETTER: A formal letter emphasizing ESL instructional strategies, experience coaching competitive debate, and long-term commitment to cross-cultural education.
• GAP ANALYSIS: A bulleted assessment identifying missing credentials, such as specific MEXT certifications, required IB professional development workshops, or niche technical skills listed in the job post.
• MITIGATION STEPS: For every item in the Gap Analysis, provide a concise one-sentence "bridge" statement to be used in the application to address how these requirements will be met.

Do not provide interview coaching, general career advice, or grading rubrics. Focus exclusively on the requested documents and the strategic gap assessment.
