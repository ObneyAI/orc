I need to spice up our internal security newsletter so people actually read it for once. Can you take these five findings and turn them into 5-7-5 haikus? I want to keep the technical gist of what's happening but please don't use the actual names of the vulnerabilities—no mention of stuff like "SQL injection" or "Misconfiguration." Just describe the behavior itself. Also, definitely don't include any fixes or tell me how bad they are; I just need the poems. Here’s the list: 1. The web application takes user-supplied input and puts it directly into a database query string, allowing unauthorized data retrieval. 2.
