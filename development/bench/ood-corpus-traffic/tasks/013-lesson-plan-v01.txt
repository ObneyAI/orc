Develop an 8-session lesson sequence for a Beginner Spanish adult evening course using the attached A1 learning objectives. Each session is 90 minutes. Resources available include a projector, whiteboards, and the 'Pasos 1' coursebook. For each session, list specific objectives, a timed breakdown of communicative activities, required materials, and a quick formative assessment method. Prioritize practical conversational skills, specifically ordering food, asking for directions, and shopping. Ensure a logical progression suited for adult learners with minimal out-of-class study time. Deliver a comprehensive unit plan only; no individual worksheets required.
