Please draft a structured weekly operations manual for our new school garden club, formatted as a step-by-step checklist. We are working with six 4x8 raised beds in a semi-arid Zone 8 climate, receiving roughly 7 hours of daily sun. Please follow these specific requirements:

1. Create a week-by-week action calendar beginning the first week of February and concluding at the end of the school year in late June.
2. Designate exact dates for both direct sowing and transplanting for our specific crop list: sugar snap peas, strawberries, cherry tomatoes, and bell peppers.
3. Develop a succession planting strategy for the pea sections, identifying specific heat-tolerant crops to replace them once temperatures rise in May.
4. Outline a liquid seaweed fertilization schedule and a deep-watering protocol that a group of students can realistically complete within two 45-minute sessions per week.
5. Provide estimated harvest windows for each vegetable so the students can track their progress and plan for a final harvest party.
6. Keep the document strictly focused on the chronological timeline of tasks; omit general gardening theory, bed construction guides, or pest/disease diagnostics.

The final output should serve as a primary directive for student volunteers to ensure the beds remain productive and well-maintained throughout the semester.
