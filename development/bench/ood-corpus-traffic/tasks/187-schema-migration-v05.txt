Please draft a comprehensive technical migration plan for transitioning our 'ProductCatalog' MySQL table from its current monolithic form to a new normalized schema. The primary goal is to extract the 'Specifications' JSON column into a dedicated 'ProductAttributes' table to improve our filtering capabilities. We are dealing with approximately 80 million rows and must maintain zero downtime despite a heavy, constant read/write load.

Your response should be structured as follows:

1. Detail the precise sequence for DDL execution and application layer deployments to ensure the transition is transparent to users.
2. Outline a robust dual-write and backfill strategy. This needs to handle incoming real-time updates while processing historical data in batches tuned to prevent replication lag or long-running table locks.
3. Provide a clear cut-over strategy and a corresponding rollback plan. Define the specific failure thresholds or triggers that would require us to abort and revert to the legacy schema.
4. Include data integrity verification queries to confirm that every row and JSON attribute has been mapped accurately to the new relational structure.

I have provided the current DDL, the proposed target schema, and recent traffic logs for context. Please focus strictly on the structural transition and data consistency; do not spend time on general query optimization or indexing beyond what is necessary for the migration itself.
