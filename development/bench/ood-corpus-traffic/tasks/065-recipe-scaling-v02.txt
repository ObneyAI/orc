BASE RECIPE (Yields 8 Whole Chickens):
- 8 Whole Chickens (approx 3lbs each)
- 25 Bird's-eye chilies, stemmed
- 12 cloves Garlic
- 1 cup Smoked Paprika
- 2 cups Lemon Juice
- 1/2 cup Dried Oregano
- 1.5 cups Olive Oil
- 4 tbsp Kosher Salt
- 2 tbsp Black Pepper
Technique: Blend marinade, coat spatchcocked birds, marinate 4 hours, grill over medium heat for 35-40 mins.
